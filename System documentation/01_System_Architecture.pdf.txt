1. Project Overview
Project Name

Daystar University Transport Management System (DTMS)

Vision

The Daystar University Transport Management System (DTMS) is a centralized digital platform designed to modernize and automate the management of university transportation services.

The objective is to replace manual passenger management, paper records, manual M-Pesa verification, and inefficient bus scheduling with a secure, scalable, and intelligent transport management platform.

The system serves students, transport administrators, drivers, and ICT while remaining scalable for future university expansion.

2. Objectives

The system aims to:

• Allow students to book buses online.

• Digitize seat allocation.

• Prevent double booking.

• Integrate M-Pesa payments.

• Generate secure QR tickets.

• Allow administrators to manage buses and trips.

• Assign buses and drivers.

• Support university event transportation.

• Produce financial and operational reports.

• Operate reliably even during temporary network outages.
