# 📘 Daystar University Bus Booking & Fleet Management System

# Logical Database Model

### Version 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Logical Database Model defines every business entity that exists inside the Daystar University Bus Booking & Fleet Management System and explains how those entities relate to one another.

Unlike the physical database, this model is independent of PostgreSQL or any other DBMS.

Its purpose is to model the business.

Only after this document is complete will we convert it into SQL tables.

---

# 2. Database Philosophy

Our database is centred around one important concept:

> **Everything revolves around a Trip.**

Not buses.

Not bookings.

Not payments.

A bus cannot exist in the booking system unless it has been assigned to a trip.

Think of it like an airline.

Passengers don't book airplanes.

They book flights.

The same principle applies here.

Students don't book buses.

Students book **Trips**.

That decision alone will make the system far easier to scale.

# 7. Business Rules

These rules govern the data model:

* A trip cannot exist without a route.
* A trip must have one assigned bus.
* A trip must have one assigned driver.
* A seat belongs to only one bus.
* A booking belongs to only one trip.
* A payment belongs to only one booking.
* A ticket is generated only after successful payment.
* Seat reservations expire automatically after 5 minutes if payment is not completed.
* Students may cancel bookings only up to 1 hour before departure.
* A ticket can be downloaded a maximum of two times.
* Special event trips may optionally allow or disallow student bookings.
* Every administrator action is recorded in the audit log.
